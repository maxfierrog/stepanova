# 0003: Design philosophy

Date: 2026-11-21. Status: proposed.

## Context

Design is not a layer applied to Stepanova after the fact; it is where the
product's philosophical foundations are decided. Every later decision about
interaction, layout, and visual language must answer to this record.

The name is deliberate. Varvara Stepanova (1894–1958) was a constructivist who
left easel painting for production art: she believed the artist should be a
constructor who organizes material into useful objects, and she worked in a
textile factory to prove it. Creativity, for her, ended not in contemplation but
in a thing in the world.

## Decision

1. **Stepanova is a medium.** It provides a space where epistemic creativity is
   turned, as efficiently as possible, into material product. Thinking is raw
   material; the system is always oriented toward what gets produced from it.

2. **Understanding leaves material behind.** Reading and thinking normally leave
   only a fading memory ("I read that once, but forgot it"). In Stepanova, the
   act of understanding produces material: selections cut from sources, links
   between them, questions put to the agent with that material as context, and
   documents constructed from all of it. Knowledge is built and kept, not
   hoped to be retained.

3. **Pragmatic, not ideological.** Stepanova takes no side in anti-AI purism or
   in the performance of analog reading. It is judged only by what turns thinking
   into knowledge most efficiently: no paper, intelligence and context and
   questions connected directly, and no need for a library one cannot afford in
   space or money. The agent is a tool of production in the workshop, like a
   loom or a press.

4. **Beauty through function.** The spirit is constructivist and brutalist. The
   essence is in the material. Something is beautiful not artificially but by its
   function, and in that it can achieve greater beauty than ornament. Anything
   that does not serve the objective is removed.

5. **Everything is exposed.** There is no hidden structure beneath a polished
   surface, and no split between primary elements and supporting structure:
   every element is structure, and all of it is visible.

6. **Brutal does not mean crowded, uncomposed, or lackluster.** Every element is
   deliberately placed and space is used with intent. The rigor of the
   composition is what makes it beautiful.

7. **The app UI is a tonal charcoal composition.** It uses black, white, and the
   greys between them, with value used to compose. Color belongs only to user
   content, such as ink and images.

## Consequences

- Interaction, layout, typography, and visual language are decided after, and
  measured against, this record. Each gets its own record.
- The exploratory Figma file created on 2026-11-21 (tokens, components, one
  screen) predates this record and made unreviewed design choices. It is a
  throwaway reference, not a source of truth, until rebuilt from these
  decisions.
